      2Bond and register

      LoomBonds · Unichain Sepolia

      One funded key runs every role. Your GPU's key is derived from it, bonds on Unichain and publishes the endpoint buyers' blobs are served from.

      cp .env.example .env                       # LOOM_PRIVATE_KEY = a testnet-only key
python -m loom preflight                   # checks RPCs and balances
python -m loom fund     --network testnet  # tops up the derived GPU keys
python -m loom register --network testnet  # bonds each GPU on Unichain

      3Serve stages

      Commits on Base Sepolia

      The node waits for the referee to schedule it, runs its slice of GPT-2 on the activations handed to it, stores the output blob and commits its hash.

      python -m loom run --network testnet       # serve stages, commit hashes, get paid

      Good to know

      • ✓Only activations, never the whole modelEach GPU runs its slice of layers on the activations handed to it.
      • ✓Paid by layers servedSettlement splits the job payment across stages by layer count, when the challenge window closes.
      • ✓Reputation decides who gets workThe referee ranks GPUs by jobs − 10 × faults − 3 × timeouts.
      • ✓Offline isn't fraudA missed deadline costs a small fixed penalty and the stage is rerouted.
      • !Cheating costs the whole bondTwo of three verifiers re-run your stage. If they agree you faked it, half the bond goes to the challenger and half to the buyer, and the address is barred.

        Agents

        One watcher audits every result. Three verifiers settle disputes by vote.

        How a check works

        From a proposed result to a decision on chain.

        1. Result proposedThe last GPU commits and a challenge window opens.
        2. Watcher re-runsIt reproduces each stage and accepts every one within tolerance.
        3. ChallengeIt posts its trace and a bond from the first stage it can't reproduce.
        4. Verifiers voteEach re-runs only that stage from its agreed input.
        5. Referee resolvesAt 2 of 3 votes, Reactive slashes, reroutes or rejects.
